How to Make Scrambled Eggs

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Milk (optional)

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Beat the eggs with a fork or whisk until well mixed.
  3. Add a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Heat a pan over medium-low heat and add some butter.
  5. Pour the beaten eggs into the pan.
  6. Stir the eggs gently with a spatula as they cook.
  7. Continue stirring until the eggs are softly scrambled and cooked through.
  8. Remove the eggs from the heat and serve.
